# Alexander Rasputin Profile Context

Use this document as source context for writing, designing, or prototyping Alexander's personal landing page. It is not final website copy. It is a compact brief about who he is, what he is strong at, and how the page should feel.

## Core Identity

Alexander Rasputin is a **Senior Product Engineer**.

He is strongest in the space between product thinking, interface craft, and engineering systems. He likes working across the path from **product idea to working experience**: shaping the direction, designing the experience, wiring the workflows behind it, and refining the details until the result is coherent and good to use.

His background includes senior frontend and product-facing engineering work, strong Flutter/mobile experience, web application development, UX-heavy implementation, and startup scale-up experience. His current scope is broader than mobile and web: he is moving into end-to-end product engineering across frontend, backend workflows, integrations, automation, desktop applications, and creative 3D/Three.js exploration.

## Short Positioning

Alexander is a senior product engineer building end-to-end product experiences with strong UX judgment, broad engineering range, and a growing practice around agentic workflows.

He is not trying to position himself as a generic full-stack engineer, a pure AI engineer, or a "vibe coder." The AI angle should support the product engineering identity: he uses agents, orchestration, and workflow design to move faster from idea to working software while still caring about craft, quality, and product judgment.

## Core Loop

Canonical phrase:

> Product idea to working experience

What that means:

- Understand the product direction and user need.
- Shape the interaction, UX, and product behavior.
- Build the interface.
- Wire the workflows, backend logic, integrations, and automation behind it.
- Verify behavior and polish details.
- Iterate until the product feels clear, useful, and good to use.

## Current Strengths

- Product-heavy engineering: enjoys shaping product decisions, UX, and implementation together.
- Interface craft: strong eye for UI, UX, detail, motion, and how software feels.
- Frontend depth: strong background in web interfaces, Angular, React, and production frontend systems.
- Flutter and mobile experience: senior Flutter/mobile work, iOS/Android knowledge, and mobile product delivery.
- End-to-end product engineering: increasingly comfortable across backend workflows, integrations, automation, and product pipelines.
- Agentic engineering: uses AI agents and orchestration to explore, implement, review, and ship faster.
- Ownership under ambiguity: good when the product, workflow, or implementation path is not fully defined yet.
- Startup scale-up experience: joined CarOnSale very early as the second engineering hire after the CTO and experienced the company growing from roughly 20 to roughly 300 people. This is strong supporting proof, but not the main public identity.
- Creative exploration: currently exploring Three.js, 3D, shaders, and more expressive web experiences.

## Recent Proof Points

- CarOnSale: senior frontend/product engineering work across mobile and web products in a fast-growing startup context.
- Bikepark Thunersee Trailpass: freelance work on a webhook pipeline that generates customer tickets, showing backend workflow and product pipeline range beyond visual frontend work.
- Ongoing personal site work: a scroll-driven, Three.js-heavy landing page that should tell a story around Alexander rather than become a static CV.

## Tone

Use a **humble builder voice**.

The page should feel confident but not loud. Avoid over-claiming, AI hype, or presenting Alexander as a one-person miracle machine. The voice should feel curious, warm, grounded, and craft-led.

Good tone:

- "I like working across..."
- "Lately, I have been exploring..."
- "I care about..."
- "I am drawn to..."
- "I use agentic workflows to move faster through the loop..."

Avoid:

- "I revolutionize..."
- "I 10x products..."
- "AI-native genius..."
- "Vibe coding..."
- Making the CarOnSale scale-up story the hero identity.

## Landing Page Direction

The site should feel like a **story-led personal profile**, not a résumé.

It should welcome people into Alexander's evolving practice: professional work, product experiments, unfinished explorations, learning, craft, and personal interests. The page can show range, but product engineering should remain the spine. It can have workshop energy in the experiments and details, but the main idea is to tell a coherent story around Alexander.

The strongest emotional center is:

> Curious product craft

The site should communicate that Alexander is a builder with taste: someone who cares about how things work, how they feel, and how ideas become usable software.

## Suggested Opening Direction

This is directional, not final copy:

> Hi, I'm Alexander, a senior product engineer exploring how to build better product experiences end to end.
>
> I care about the space where product thinking, interface craft, and engineering systems meet: shaping ideas, designing the experience, wiring the workflows behind it, and polishing the details until it feels good to use.
>
> Lately, I have been deepening my work with agentic workflows: using AI orchestration to move faster through that whole loop while staying intentional about craft, quality, and product judgment.

## AI / Agentic Engineering Angle

AI should be framed as a workflow advantage first and a product-building identity second.

Hero-level framing:

> Agentic workflows help me move faster through the product-building loop.

Deeper-page framing:

> I use AI orchestration to explore product directions, prototype interfaces, write and review code, verify behavior, and ship complete product slices faster.

Important: this is not "vibe coding." It is agentic engineering: structured, reviewed, orchestrated software work with human product judgment in the loop.

## Personal Craft Layer

Personal interests should appear as subtle texture, not as a separate lifestyle brand.

Alexander is also drawn to surfing, photography, woodcrafting, and other hands-on creative practices. These interests matter because they explain taste, attention, patience, material awareness, and curiosity.

Possible direction:

> Outside of software, I am drawn to things that require attention and feel: surfing, photography, woodcrafting, and small experiments that teach me how materials, light, motion, and tools behave.

Keep this light and visible, but not dominant.

## What The Page Should Not Be

- Not a CV with decorative visuals.
- Not a generic developer portfolio.
- Not a freelancer sales page.
- Not an AI hype page.
- Not only "mobile and web."
- Not only "frontend."
- Not a case-study archive before the core story is clear.
- Not a maker-studio or workshop concept that distracts from Alexander's story.

## What The Page Should Be

- A story-led personal profile.
- A product engineer's home base.
- A place where shipped work, experiments, and craft can coexist.
- A visual and interactive proof of taste.
- A clear signal that Alexander can move from product idea to working experience.
- Humble, curious, warm, and technically capable.
